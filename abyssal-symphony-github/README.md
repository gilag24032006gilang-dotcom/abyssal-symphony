# Abyssal Symphony by Team Lucidance

Dance with the living light of the deep sea. Move in front of your webcam or play any song, and bioluminescent creatures answer in light, each with the real science behind it.

**Try it:** open this site in Chrome or Edge on a laptop with a webcam, and allow camera access.

Submitted to the **OceanX Education Impact Challenge 2026**, Track 1 "Living Light".
