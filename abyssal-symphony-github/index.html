About the experience
  • The showA deep-sea dance troupe

    You are the lead dancer. Sea pens, sea fireflies, lanternfish and crystal jellies take turns with the rhythm, then gather for the finale.

  • Stomp & jumpSea Pens

    Waves of light ripple through the sea-pen garden from where your feet land.

  • Raise your hands highSea Fireflies

    Strings of glowing pearls rise higher and brighter. Clap, and they release their pearls all at once.

  • Crouch & standCrystal Jellies

    The jelly formation sinks and rises with your body. Touch a bell and it sparkles.

  • Swing your armsLanternfish

    The school follows the trail of your hands. Zigzag, and the school scatters, then regroups.

  • Use musicThe avatar dances on its own

    Upload a song: the avatar improvises with moves learned from professional dancers (9 styles), following the tempo, energy and mood of the music, while every creature answers on the beat.

  • Whole bodyCurrents of Light

    Lean, spin, stomp, jump: the water follows your body.

Press F for fullscreen, M to mute, or Esc to stop. Music and camera stay on this device.

Music-mode movement comes from the AIST++ Dance Motion Dataset (Google, CC BY 4.0; Li et al. 2021), recomposed live to your song.
